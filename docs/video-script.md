# Helvaro promotion video — script & prompts

Een complete 60-seconden explainer in 6 scenes van 8 seconden.
Elke scene is een aparte Higgsfield-prompt klaar om te kopiëren.

> **Aanrader:** model `veo3_1_lite`, 8s, 16:9, 1080p, no audio (8 credits / scene).
> Voor 9:16 versie voor TikTok/Reels: alle prompts met `aspect_ratio: "9:16"`.

---

## Stijl-gids (gebruik in elke prompt)

```
2D motion graphics animation, flat illustration style with smooth
transitions. Modern SaaS commercial aesthetic. Brand palette:
deep navy #050811 background, electric purple #6366f1 accents,
mint green #22c55e for success states, white typography.
Clean, minimal, professional — like Notion or Linear ads.
No realistic humans, no photographs.
```

## Scene 1 (0-8s) — The hook: a frustrated business owner ★ Hero clip

```
2D motion graphics animation, flat illustration style. Modern SaaS
commercial aesthetic. Brand palette: deep navy #050811 background,
electric purple #6366f1, mint green accents, white typography.

A stylized smartphone tilts in from the right showing a contact form
titled "Helvaro" with two input fields. A finger taps the purple
"Stuur" button. The form smoothly morphs into a WhatsApp chat
interface. A green chat bubble slides up from the bottom with text
"Hey 👋 ik ben Sara van Acme..." followed by three animated typing
dots. Three more bubbles cascade in showing qualifying questions
(budget icon, calendar icon, location pin). Camera glides left to
reveal a Calendly-style calendar widget — a time slot fills with
bright green and a large checkmark animation appears.

End frame: bold typography "Helvaro — je AI sales-assistent" with
the Helvaro wordmark in a purple gradient. Subtle particle effects,
glowing accents, professional motion graphics quality.
```

## Scene 2 (8-16s) — The problem statement

```
2D motion graphics animation, flat illustration style, deep navy
background. A stopwatch icon animates ticking through "30 minutes",
"1 hour", "next day" — meanwhile a stylized lead-card (with a name
and phone icon) fades from green to grey to red, representing a lead
going cold. A red downward arrow appears with text "60% van leads
verdwijnen na 1 uur" in large white sans-serif type.

Cut: a calendar with 30 slot icons appears, half of them flash red
(missed). Camera pushes in on a single red slot which morphs into
a Euro symbol €. Text appears: "Hoeveel deals laat jij liggen?"

Style: motion graphics, smooth transitions, dramatic but clean.
Subtle red glow on the missed leads, no humans.
```

## Scene 3 (16-24s) — The solution introduction

```
2D motion graphics animation, flat illustration style. Background
shifts from deep navy to a softer dark blue with purple radial glow.
The Helvaro wordmark assembles letter by letter at the top.

Centerpiece: a stylized AI assistant character (geometric humanoid
silhouette in purple gradient, NOT photorealistic — abstract shape
only) materializes with a soft glow. Around the AI, three icon
satellites orbit: a WhatsApp green bubble, a Calendly calendar, a
notification bell — all connected with thin glowing purple lines
forming a network diagram.

Below the AI character, text fades in: "Beantwoordt + kwalificeert +
boekt — automatisch, 24/7". The icons pulse in rhythm with the AI's
soft glow. Floating particles around the scene. Clean professional
animation.
```

## Scene 4 (24-32s) — The product demo (WhatsApp conversation)

```
2D motion graphics animation, flat illustration style. Smartphone
mockup centered on dark navy background, screen showing a live
WhatsApp chat thread.

Animation sequence: messages appear one by one from bottom with
spring physics —
1. (left, grey bubble) "Hoi, ik heb interesse in een woning in Gent"
2. (right, green bubble with Sara avatar circle) "Hey 👋 Wat is je
   budget en wanneer wil je verhuizen?"
3. (left, grey) "300k tot 400k, binnen 3 maanden"
4. (right, green) "Top! Mag ik je morgen 10u bellen?"
5. (left, grey) "Ja graag"

After the last bubble, a Calendly card slides up overlaying the
phone — a calendar slot turns bright green with checkmark animation
and text "Afspraak ingepland ✓".

Side annotation tags float in: "1 min reactietijd", "AI kwalificeert",
"Auto-geboekt". Motion graphics, smooth, professional.
```

## Scene 5 (32-40s) — The dashboard reveal

```
2D motion graphics animation, flat illustration style, isometric
perspective. A dark navy laptop screen rises from the bottom of the
frame, tilted in isometric view. On the screen: the Helvaro
dashboard mockup with three stat cards animating in sequence:
"12 leads vandaag", "9 gekwalificeerd (75%)", "5 afspraken geboekt".

Below the stats, a lead-list scrolls smoothly downward showing
animated lead cards with green checkmarks and stars. On the right,
a chat-preview panel shows a WhatsApp conversation snippet animating
in real-time.

Around the laptop, floating UI elements: a phone showing a WhatsApp
notification, a calendar with green slots, an envelope icon. All
elements pulse softly with purple glow. Text overlay: "Jij ziet
enkel de warme prospects."

Camera slowly orbits 15 degrees around the isometric laptop.
```

## Scene 6 (40-48s) — The transformation + CTA

```
2D motion graphics animation, flat illustration style. Split-screen
comparison animation: left side labeled "Zonder Helvaro" shows a
chaotic stack of red lead-cards with stopwatch icons, slowly falling
and fading. Right side labeled "Met Helvaro" shows neatly organized
green lead-cards stacking up, each one with a green checkmark and
Euro symbol.

The split-screen merges into a single hero shot: the Helvaro wordmark
appears large and centered in purple gradient with a subtle glow.
Below it, the tagline "Je AI sales-assistent — 24/7 op WhatsApp"
in clean white sans-serif.

Final frame: URL text "helvaro.pro" appears at the bottom with a
purple gradient underline drawing in. A CTA button "Gratis demo →"
pulses softly. Subtle confetti or particle effects.

Background: deep navy with a soft purple radial gradient.
```
