Agent 03 of ten

Not every buyer knows yet which car they want.

Some of your visitors search by requirements rather than by model: automatic, around thirty thousand, not too many kilometres. Answering that question costs your salesperson ten minutes of searching, so it often does not happen.

Book a demo → This is how the system fits together
Without this

A search filter is not a salesperson.

Your website has a filter with make, price and fuel. Anyone who knows exactly what they want will manage. Anyone who does not clicks three times and leaves.

A buyer who says "I'm looking for an automatic around thirty thousand with under a hundred thousand kilometres" is really asking a sales question. It is just that at that moment nobody is there to answer it.

And this is exactly the kind of buyer not yet tied to one business. Whoever helps them choose, sells.

  • Visitors who find nothing do not come back.
  • Vehicles that do not fit the first filter never get looked at.
  • Your salesperson looks up the same thing three times a day.
The workflow

What the system does.

From requirements to three vehicles that are actually there.

  1. 01

    Asking what they are looking for

    Budget, body style, fuel, transmission, mileage and what they use the car for. Four questions, not a twenty-field form.

  2. 02

    Searching your stock

    Only vehicles that are actually there. No models you might be able to order and no cars sold last week.

  3. 03

    Showing a few, not thirty

    Three to five vehicles with the data that decides the choice. A list of thirty is the same as no answer.

  4. 04

    Explaining why

    Why these three fit their requirements, and where they differ. That is the part a filter does not do.

  5. 05

    Continuing where they choose

    If they pick one, the conversation continues as a normal enquiry: questions, appointment, file.

  6. 06

    Finding nothing is also an answer

    If nothing fits, that gets said. With the question whether they want a message when something does come in.

BuyerThat Volvo looks good.

Limits

What this agent does not do.

  • It only shows vehicles that are in your stock.
  • It does not promise a vehicle that is still to arrive.
  • It gives no technical advice on reliability or consumption that is not in your data.
  • It does not negotiate and gives no discount to force a choice.
