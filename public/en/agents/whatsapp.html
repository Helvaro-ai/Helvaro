Home · Agents · WhatsApp
Agent 08 of ten

On WhatsApp people answer wel.

A buyer does not call back and does not read their email, but answers a message within the hour. That is why this is the channel where conversations continue and appointments get pinned down.

Book a demo → This is how the system fits together
Without this

The fastest channel is the worst organised one.

WhatsApp usually sits on one salesperson's phone. If they are off, the conversation stalls. If they leave, the customer contact goes with them.

There is no file, no overview and no handover. What was agreed sits in a chat nobody else can reach.

At the same time it is the channel buyers respond fastest on. That makes it too important to hang off one phone.

  • Customer contact that lives on a private phone is not company property.
  • A colleague cannot take over, because they cannot see the conversation.
  • Appointments sit in a chat instead of in a calendar.
The workflow

What the system does.

The same system, on the channel where your buyer already is.

  1. 01

    One number for the business

    Not a salesperson's phone, but a company number everyone can reach and where everything is kept.

  2. 02

    The conversation starts with the vehicle

    If they come from an advert, the vehicle is known straight away. If not, further questions follow.

  3. 03

    Answering and asking further

    Availability, data, and the questions that follow. In the same tone your business would use itself.

  4. 04

    Photos both ways

    Extra pictures of the vehicle to the buyer, photos of their trade-in to you. On WhatsApp that happens by itself.

  5. 05

    Pinning down the appointment

    With a confirmation and a reminder the day before, in the same chat.

  6. 06

    Your salesperson reads along

    They watch the conversation run and step in when needed. After that they can let it run again.

This is how it sounds

A conversation that comes out of this.

An example, not a recording of a customer. You set the tone yourself, this one is ours.

WhatsApp · from an advertExample
BuyerHi, I saw the Golf GTI. Still available?
HelvaroYes, the 2022 Golf GTI with 41,000 km is still here, €34,500.
BuyerHas it had any damage?
HelvaroOur data shows no damage history. The full history is with the vehicle, you can look at it on site.
BuyerAnd can I drive it on Saturday?
HelvaroSaturday works at 10:00 or 13:30. Bring your driving licence.
Buyer10:00 works 👍
Test drive pinned down Saturday 10:00 · Golf GTI · confirmation sent
What it produces

The channel they actually answer on.

Buyers respond faster

A message gets read. A voicemail and an email often do not.

The conversation stays the company's

Not on a salesperson's phone, but in a file your colleagues can reach.

Handing over costs nothing

Whoever takes over sees the whole conversation. The buyer does not notice the switch.

Photos are no hassle

Pictures of the vehicle or of their trade-in go back and forth without anyone having to ask for an email address.

Limits

What this agent does not do.

  • It sends no messages to people who did not get in touch themselves.
  • It does not use WhatsApp to advertise to your whole database.
  • It invents no vehicle data, not even when the buyer pushes.
  • A conversation that gets out of hand goes straight to a person.
