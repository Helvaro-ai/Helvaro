The workflows

Eight workflows. One system underneath.

These are not eight separate products. They are eight routes through the same system: same buyer, same vehicle, same file. You switch on what you need and the rest stands ready.

Book a demo → See how it works
Why this hangs together

Eight separate tools never add up to a sales process.

You can buy something separate for each of these tasks. Then you have eight subscriptions, eight inboxes and zero overview of what happened with one buyer.

One buyer

They email tonight and message tomorrow. That is the same person with the same question, not two separate conversations.

One vehicle

Which car it is gets established once and stays the same everywhere after that. Including for the trade-in and the financing.

One file

Everything that has been said sits in one place. Your salesperson never has to ask again what was already discussed.

One set of rules

What the system may do on its own and what goes past a salesperson first you set once. That then applies to all eight.

Group 1

From question to answer.

What happens in the first minutes after a buyer gets in touch. This is where most enquiries are won or lost.

01New enquiry Is it still there, what does it cost, when can I come and look. Answered with the data from your stock, also at a quarter to ten in the evening. → 03Vehicle advice A buyer searching on requirements instead of a model gets three vehicles that are actually there, with the difference between them explained. →
Group 2

From answer to appointment.

Three workflows around the questions that stand between interest and a signature: driving, trading in and paying.

02Test drive First check whether the vehicle is free, only then name a slot. With confirmation, reminder and the vehicle attached to the appointment. → 04Trade-in Make, year, mileage, history and photos in before they pull up. Your salesperson sets the figure. → 05Financing "What does that cost per month" is a buying signal. It gets explained and passed on, not brushed aside and not promised. →
Group 3

Where it happens, and what comes after that.

Two channels and the follow-up attached to them. On all three the same file and the same vehicle.

08WhatsApp The channel buyers answer fastest on, on one business number instead of one salesperson's phone. → 07Email The longest and often most serious enquiries. Read, linked to a vehicle and answered before they sink into the mailbox. → 06Follow-up Three contact moments after a conversation went quiet, with the vehicle attached. Then clarity, not endless repetition. →
Advice

Start with one.

Switching everything on at once sounds efficient and rarely works. You cannot tell what is working, your salespeople lose the overview, and the first mistake in one workflow puts your whole company on edge. Switch on one, measure for four weeks, then the next.

  • Do you not know where to startThen the new enquiry, because there you see straight away how much comes in outside your hours.
  • If a lot of enquiries come in by emailThen email, because that is where the longest and often most serious enquiry is waiting.
  • If conversations often go quietThen the follow-up, because that is where work you have already done is lying around.
