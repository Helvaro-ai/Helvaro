Agent 07 of ten

The serious enquiries still come in by email.

An email is longer, more often mentions a trade-in and more often comes from someone who means it. And it is exactly that email that sinks between invoices, newsletters and suppliers.

Book a demo → This is how the system fits together
Without this

A good enquiry disappears into a shared mailbox.

Everything arrives in a dealership's general mailbox: enquiries, invoices, notifications from classifieds sites and advertising. An enquiry that lands there at a quarter past eight in the evening sits under twenty other messages the next morning.

There is no owner. Everyone assumes someone else will pick it up, and that is exactly what does not happen.

On top of that such an email often says more than a chat message: which vehicle, what they drive now, when they are free. That is free information going unused.

  • The best enquiry of the week becomes the slowest answered.
  • Nobody feels ownership, so it stays lying there.
  • What the email says never gets put into a file.
The workflow

What the system does.

From a message in a mailbox to a file with a next step.

  1. 01

    The email gets read

    Enquiries are told apart from invoices and advertising. Only what is about a vehicle enters the flow.

  2. 02

    Picking out the vehicle

    From the link, the advert number or the description. If it is not unambiguous, a question comes back.

  3. 03

    Filtering the rest out

    Trade-in, budget, term, when they are free. What the email says, nobody has to ask again.

  4. 04

    Answering in the same tone

    An email deserves an email, not a chat message. With the vehicle's data and a concrete proposal in it.

  5. 05

    Creating a file

    Even if they continue on WhatsApp later, it stays the same file with the same vehicle.

  6. 06

    Follow up

    If it stays quiet, it goes to the follow-up, just as with any other channel.

Limits

What this agent does not do.

  • It does not answer email that is not about a vehicle.
  • It invents no data that is not in your stock.
  • It names no trade-in figure.
  • An angry or legal email goes straight to a person.
