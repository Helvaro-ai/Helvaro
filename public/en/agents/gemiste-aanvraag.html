Agent 06 of ten

The conversation does not stop when the buyer stops typing.

"I'll think about it" is not a no. It is the point where nearly every dealership stops, because following up hangs on someone's memory and that memory has other things to do.

Book a demo → This is how the system fits together
Without this

Following up is work that never becomes urgent enough.

A buyer asks about a vehicle, gets an answer, says they will think about it and disappears. There is no rejection, there is only silence. And silence is on nobody's to-do list.

Three days later nobody remembers who it was, which vehicle it concerned or what was agreed. The enquiry still exists in a mailbox, but it is no longer alive.

Whoever sends a short message on day three with the right vehicle attached wins some of those buyers back. That happens almost nowhere, and that is exactly why it works.

  • A buyer who was wavering buys elsewhere without you noticing.
  • The work that went into the first conversation yields nothing.
  • Nobody knows which enquiries are still open and which are dead.
The workflow

What the system does.

Three contact moments, in your tone, and then clarity.

  1. 01

    Silence gets noticed

    A conversation without a follow-up gets a status and a date. That way it is no longer in a mailbox but on a list.

  2. 02

    After two days: something useful

    Not "have you thought about it yet", but something that helps them along. The vehicle is still there, or there are extra photos.

  3. 03

    After a week: a slot

    A concrete proposal to come and look or drive. That makes yes easier than postponing once more.

  4. 04

    After two weeks: close it off neatly

    A last message saying they can always reply, and that the vehicle may have been sold in the meantime. After that it stops.

  5. 05

    The vehicle sets the tone

    If the vehicle has been sold in the meantime, that gets said, with an alternative from your stock alongside.

  6. 06

    Every enquiry gets an ending

    Appointment, dropped out or expired. No more list of things nobody knows are still alive.

What it produces

Buyers you already had.

The work is already done

The first conversation has happened, the vehicle is known. Whatever still becomes an appointment costs almost nothing extra.

Doubt is not a rejection

Between "I'll think about it" and a no there is room. Almost nobody uses that room.

A no is a gain too

Then you know that vehicle is free again for someone else and you stop counting on it.

Nobody has to chase by phone

The most awkward call of the week disappears from your salesperson's to-do list.

Limits

What this agent does not do.

  • It stops after three messages. Keeping on pushing does not produce appointments.
  • It approaches nobody who has said they do not want messages.
  • It does not give away a discount to push someone over the line.
  • It does not promise a vehicle will stay available.
