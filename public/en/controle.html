Control

Your team decides. The system does the groundwork.

A system that talks to buyers on its own without you seeing what happens is exactly what we do not build. Six agreements fixed before we start.

01

Your salesperson takes over

At any moment a salesperson steps into the conversation and the system stops answering. The buyer notices no handover, they simply get an answer from a person.

02

Every conversation readable

You read every conversation live and in full afterwards. Export whenever you want. A summary is never the only thing left.

03

Approval per action type

You decide what the system may do on its own and what goes past a salesperson first. Per type, not one switch for everything. Naming a price is not the same as pinning down a test drive.

04

Action log

What was done, on which vehicle, when, and who approved it. Exportable, also when your insurer or your auditor asks for it.

05

Data in the EU

Processing within the EU, a published sub-processor list, a standard data processing agreement and a retention period you set yourself.

06

Emergency stop

You stop every workflow yourself, immediately, without calling us. Per workflow, not everything at once.

What we do not claim: that a mistake is never made. That is why your salesperson can take over and why the approvals above exist. Our model providers process approved context under their own terms, and we put that in writing before we start.

Twenty minutes, on your own numbers.

We look together at what comes in at your place and where it gets stuck. You leave with a first workflow, or with the reason why it does not fit you.
