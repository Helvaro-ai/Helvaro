For dealerships

Built for the dealership where salespeople are out on the floor.

Forty to three hundred vehicles on the forecourt, two to eight salespeople and a website more people look at in the evening than come onto the floor during the day. That is the business this is meant for.

Book a demo → Work it out first
This fits
  • Independent dealerships and used-car traders
  • Businesses with forty to three hundred vehicles in stock
  • Sales teams of two to eight people
  • Businesses that advertise on portals and on their own site
  • Those who get enough enquiries but convert too few of them
This does not fit
  • Franchised dealers with their own contact centre and fixed scripts
  • Groups that want to steer everything centrally from one system
  • Traders who only sell to other traders
  • Anyone looking for a free chatbot for the website

If you are in this list, we would rather say so in the first conversation than in month three.

A Thursday

This is what an ordinary day looks like.

On the left what comes in. On the right what happens to it without a system behind it.

07:20

An enquiry from last night

At 23:40 someone asked through the form whether the Passat is still there and what it costs. The email sits under six others.

Without

At 23:55 they asked two other businesses the same thing. One of them answered within the hour.

10:05

Three enquiries while you are on the floor

Your salesperson is out on a test drive with a customer. Meanwhile two messages come in through the site and one via WhatsApp.

Without

They get answered at the end of the afternoon. Two out of three no longer respond by then.

11:40

"What is my car worth?"

A trade-in question with no registration, no mileage and no photos. It takes three emails back and forth before you can say anything.

Without

After the second email they drop out. You never find out which vehicle they meant.

14:15

"What does that cost per month?"

A buying signal, in the middle of a conversation about a €24,500 vehicle. The answer calls for data you do not have yet.

Without

The answer is "we'll call you back about that". Which happens two days later, or not at all.

17:50

Fourteen conversations that went quiet

People who talked about a vehicle last week and then went silent. They are on nobody's list.

Without

There is no follow-up. The work to bring them in was already done and evaporates anyway.

21:30

The busiest two hours of your website

People are on the sofa looking at the vehicles you have on your forecourt. They have questions and nobody to ask them.

Without

At half past eight tomorrow morning the forms come in. At 21:45 they could already have had an appointment somewhere.

Six moments on a single Thursday. None of them is about too little interest, and none of them can be fixed by working harder.

Your tone, not ours

Limits

What this does not do.

Every supplier has a list of capabilities. This list is more useful, because it decides whether you are disappointed after three months.

  • No price dealsThere is no negotiating and not a euro comes off the asking price. That stays with your salesperson.
  • No trade-in figureThe data gets collected, the figure comes from a person. A number out of a system cannot be walked back later.
  • No vehicle data out of thin airWhat is not in your stock is not claimed. Better "I'll check that" than the wrong trim.
  • No replacement for your salespeopleIt takes over the repetitive work. The conversations with the money in them still end up with a person.
  • No phoneWebsite, WhatsApp and email. The phone stays yours, we do nothing with it.
  • No promised figuresWe quote no percentage we have not measured at your business. How we do count is on the numbers page.
