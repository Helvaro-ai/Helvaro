Design

What the integration does.

The right vehicle attached

From a link, an advert number or a description. If it is not unambiguous, further questions follow instead of guessing.

Answers from your own data

Price, year, mileage, fuel, transmission and equipment. Exactly as you recorded them.

Searching on requirements

A buyer searching on budget and body style gets vehicles that are actually there instead of a filter.

Sold is sold

A vehicle that is gone is no longer offered. That saves you the most awkward email you can send.

State of play live

This works today with a stock file you supply, for example an export or a feed you already use for your classifieds. A direct integration with your own system is in development.

What is already possible today
  • A stock file you supply, refreshed periodically
  • The feed you already use for classifieds sites
  • Answering with price, mileage and trim from that file
  • Searching on budget, body style, fuel and mileage
What is not yet possible
  • Reading directly in your own stock system
  • Reserving a vehicle or changing its status
  • Filling in data missing from your file from another source

The better your stock data is filled in, the better the answers. Missing fields are never filled in with an estimate.

Questions we get

Short and honest.

What if a field is empty?

Then the system says it does not know and passes the question to a salesperson. It fills nothing in.

How often is the stock refreshed?

That depends on how you supply it. With a daily feed it is daily; with an integration later on, continuously.

Do we have to switch to something else?

No. We work with the file or the feed you already have.

What happens to that data?

They are processed inside the EU and used only to answer enquiries. Details are in the privacy policy.
