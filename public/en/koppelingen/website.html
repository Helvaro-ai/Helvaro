Design

What the integration does.

Watching along on the page

If the visitor is on a vehicle page, the conversation is about that one. They do not have to explain which car they mean.

Answering questions

Availability, data, opening hours and what a visit involves.

Asking for contact details when it fits

When there is genuine buying interest, and then an email address or a phone number is enough. Not both, and not from someone who is only browsing.

Handing over to another channel

If they want to continue on WhatsApp, the conversation comes along. With the vehicle and everything already said.

State of play live

This is running. It is one script on your existing site; nothing to migrate and your CMS stays what it is.

What is already possible today
  • A conversation on every page of your site
  • The vehicle on the page as context
  • Asking for contact details on buying interest
  • Switching to WhatsApp while keeping the conversation
What is not yet possible
  • Replacing a full stock module on your site
  • Changing your website's appearance
  • Payments or reservations with a deposit

You set the tone and the opening line yourself. A village garage sounds different from a franchised dealer, and so it should.

Questions we get

Short and honest.

Does our site get slower?

The script loads separately from your page. Your visitor sees your site as always, and the conversation comes afterwards.

Do we have to build anything?

Paste one line into your website. We set up the rest.

Is everyone asked for their details?

No. Anyone just browsing is left alone. That pays off better than pushing a form at everyone.

Can we switch it off?

Yes, immediately and without calling us. That goes for every channel.
