Faro

The face of the system. Not the system itself.

Helvaro is what you buy. Faro is how you operate it: the place where you read along, take over and ask in plain language what happened with a buyer.

Meet Faro

Your workflows do the work. Faro keeps watch over it.

The layer above your workflows

Faro is not a separate workflow and not a separate product. It is the layer where your workflows share their memory, where what they may do is set out, and where you see what happened. One place for every buyer and every vehicle.

  • One view of what is running Every workflow, every conversation, every vehicle. You read along live and your salesperson takes over at any moment.
  • What is waiting on you sits separately Everything needing approval lands in one list, with the reason attached. You approve it or you hold it.
  • Shared memory A correction you make once then applies to every workflow. You do not explain the same thing twice.
  • Just ask it Which enquiries sat there yesterday, which buyer is closest to a signature, which vehicle is asked about most. You ask Faro in plain language.

The workflows do the work. Faro shows what happened. Your salesperson decides.
