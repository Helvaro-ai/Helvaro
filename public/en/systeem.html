How it works

Between your buyer and your salesperson there is nobody right now.

Most dealerships do not have a gap in their advertising. They have a gap between the moment a buyer asks a question and the moment someone has time to answer. Helvaro sits in that gap.

Five layers

Where a chatbot stops, the rest begins.

The first three layers you can buy from dozens of suppliers. The last two decide whether anything actually changes in your sales.

01

Capture

Nothing comes in without someone responding.

A dealership does not have one channel. A form comes in from your site, a WhatsApp from an advert and an email into the general mailbox. At Helvaro those three come together in one flow, with one history per buyer.

The point is not that answers come faster. The point is that there always gets answered. An enquiry from 21:43 is no longer a missed enquiry, but a conversation that started at 21:43.

Channels
  • Website live
  • WhatsApp live
  • Email in development

Telephony is deliberately not listed. It is not part of the product today, and we will only put it there when it is.

02

Understand

The car as well, not just the question.

This is where the difference sits between a general assistant and something that belongs in the car trade. "Is that blue X5 still there" is a sentence to a chatbot. To this system it is a reference to a specific vehicle from your stock, with a price, a mileage and a trim.

After that the question is no longer "what does this person want", but whether this is about availability, a test drive, a trade-in or financing. That distinction sets which questions follow and when a salesperson is needed.

What is on the table
  • Which vehicle from your stock
  • Price, mileage, trim
  • What the buyer asks
  • Budget and term, if they mention them
  • Trade-in, if it comes up

Everything comes from your own data. If it is not in there, it gets asked rather than filled in.

03

Act

Answering is the beginning, not the end.

A conversation that ends with "give us a call" has solved nothing, it has moved the work to the buyer. Helvaro proposes a slot, pins it down, confirms it and reminds about it. If it stays quiet, another message follows, then one more, and then it stops.

What does not fit goes on to a person. A negotiation, a trade-in figure, a complaint. Those go to a named person at your business, with the conversation, so nobody has to start again.

What happens
  • Answering and asking further
  • Proposing a slot and pinning it down
  • Confirm and remind
  • Following up until a yes or no is on the table
  • Handing over with the whole conversation attached

What the system may do on its own and what goes past you first you set per action type. That is under control.

04

Connect

Your stock is the source, not a copy.

This is the layer where most standalone tools fall over. A chat window that does not know your stock can only say general things. And an appointment that only exists in a separate screen has to be retyped by someone. That happens for two weeks and then stops.

We only call live what is live. The rest is marked as in development or planned, and that only changes when it works at a real dealership.

State of play
  • Stock through a file live
  • Google Calendar live
  • Stock through an integration in development
  • Your company's CRM planned

All the details are on the integrations page, including what is not there yet.

05

Measure

What you do not count does not improve.

The question a dealer principal asks after three months is not "does the technology work". It is "did it earn me anything". For that you need to know how many enquiries came in, how many became appointments and how many of those would have leaked away without this system.

That last number is the only one that really matters, and it is also the hardest. That is why the pilot starts with one figure instead of a screen full of charts.

What gets counted
  • Enquiries per channel and per hour
  • What became a file
  • What became an appointment
  • Follow-up still running
  • Takeovers by a salesperson

Exactly what we measure in the pilot is on the numbers page.
