Numbers

One number, measured at your business. Not somebody else's average.

Most sites of this kind show percentages without a source and happy customers without a surname. That is no use to you, because you do not know how it was counted. We would rather measure at your business, on one number you can check yourself. This page explains which number that is and how we count it.

Sign up for the pilot → Run the numbers on your own situation
What we do say
  • The system is running and you can talk to it today
  • WhatsApp, website and form are live
  • Appointments land in Google Calendar
  • You read back every conversation and stop every workflow yourself
  • What is still being built is marked as in development
What we do not say
  • That we raise your turnover by a particular percentage
  • That a mistake is never made
  • That an integration is finished before it works at a dealership
  • Names of customers who did not give us permission for it
  • That this fits every garage

This list probably costs us customers. It also brings us better ones.

The pilot

One number. Four weeks. Counted at your business.

Not an elaborate study with twenty variables. One measurement we both understand and that cannot afterwards be explained away as chance.

Week 0

Baseline

For two weeks we count what comes in and what happens to it, with nothing switched on. How many enquiries came in, how many went unanswered, how many became an appointment. This figure is the starting point.

Week 1

One workflow switched on

Not everything at once. We switch on the workflow with the biggest gap, usually the new enquiry or the follow-up. The rest stays off, so the effect can be attributed.

Weeks 2 to 5

Measuring and adjusting

Every week we read conversations back and adjust what goes wrong. You see exactly what was said and what was scheduled.

Week 6

The number

How many of your enquiries became an appointment, compared with the baseline. If it disappoints, we write that down. That is the agreement.

The only number that counts

How many enquiries became an appointment, before and after?

Not satisfaction, not response time, not the number of messages. One ratio, measured the same way before and after, at the same business.

The deal

What you bring in and what you get back.

You get back

We guide a limited number of businesses at a time, because reading along and adjusting does not work otherwise. If it does not fit you, we say so in the first conversation.

Later on this page

What belongs in a case.

That way you can judge for yourself whether a case holds up. If one of these six is missing, it is not a case but a testimonial.

  • The situationWhich business, how many salespeople, how many vehicles in stock, what went wrong.
  • The baselineThe real numbers from before we started.
  • What was switched onWhich workflow, from when, and what was not.
  • The resultThe same measurement afterwards, with the same method.
  • What it producedIn euros where that can honestly be said.
  • The owner himselfWith their name, a photo on their own forecourt and their own words.
