How this is calculated

unanswered enquiries per week × 4.33 weeks × your conversion × your gross margin per car

This is an estimate, not a promise. It calculates with numbers you fill in yourself, and with the assumption that an enquiry you answer too late had the same chance as one you pick up straight away. In practice that is never exactly right. Use it to see an order of magnitude, not to build a business case.

What we do not count: trade-ins you miss because the data never came in, financing questions that died, test drives nobody confirmed and buyers who dropped out at the third question. Those are not in here, while together at many dealerships they are bigger than the enquiries left lying around.

The rest of the leak

Unanswered enquiries are the easiest part to count.

They are also rarely the biggest. These four are harder to measure and usually more expensive.

Conversations that went quiet

The interest was already there and the work to create it is already paid for. One more follow-up means margin straight away, because nothing new has to be added.

Trade-ins you miss out on

Anyone trading their car in with you almost always buys one too. Every trade-in question that dies at the asking stage costs you two margins at once.

Financing that dies

"What does that cost per month" is the clearest buying signal there is. Let a day pass over it and it is usually no longer a signal.

Test drives that do not happen

Whoever sits behind the wheel buys more often. An appointment that is not confirmed or for which the vehicle is not ready is a lost sales conversation.
