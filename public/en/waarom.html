The problem, the product, the numbers and an honest comparison. Everything you want to know before booking a call.

The problem

The problem isn't your team. It's timing.

Without Helvaro

Saturday, 21:47

A buyer asks on your website whether the BMW X5 is still available. He sends the same question to two other dealerships.

Saturday, 21:52

Nobody answers. He carries on chatting with the dealership that does reply.

Monday, 09:04

Your salesperson sends an email. By then he has already booked a test drive, somewhere else.

With Helvaro

Saturday, 21:47

The same buyer asks the same question.

Saturday, 21:48

Helvaro answers with the details from your stock: the X5 is still there, 82,000 km, €48,900. Then it asks about trade-in and budget.

Saturday, 21:53

The viewing is in your calendar, with the car and the trade-in attached. You read it on Monday morning over coffee.

Companies that respond within an hour qualify up to 7× more leads than companies that wait longer.
Source: Harvard Business Review, Lead Response Management Study.

How it works

Three steps. No manual work.

The first answer, the questions and the booking happen on their own. Your salesperson takes over where the money is.

1

3

The appointment is booked
